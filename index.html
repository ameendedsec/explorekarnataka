About Karnataka

Karnataka, situated in southwestern India, is a land of vivid contrasts and deep historical roots. Celebrated as the "Cradle of Stone Architecture," the state is adorned with world-renowned UNESCO monuments, intricately carved temples, and majestic palaces.

From the fast-paced tech innovation of Bengaluru to the quiet, pristine coastlines of Gokarna; from the regal charm of Mysuru to the awe-inspiring boulder landscapes of Hampi — Karnataka offers a rich tapestry of experiences for every traveler.

Flanked by the bio-diverse Western Ghats, the state is blanketed with lush coffee plantations, gushing waterfalls, and protected wildlife reserves. It is equally celebrated for its flavorful culinary traditions and vibrant hospitality.

Popular Tourist Places

Must-visit destinations that define the magic of Karnataka

Hampi

A UNESCO World Heritage Site with monumental ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire, set amidst stunning rocky hills and riverside banana groves.

Gokarna

A tranquil coastal town famous for its crescent beaches like Om Beach and Kudle Beach, blending spiritual tranquility with relaxed coastal charm.

Coorg (Kodagu)

Affectionately named the "Scotland of India," Coorg is renowned for fragrant coffee plantations, misty valleys, and unique Kodava culture.

Mysuru (Mysore)

The cultural heart of the state, famed for the magnificent Mysore Palace, the world-famous Dasara festivities, and fragrant sandalwood.

Murudeshwar

Home to the world's second tallest statue of Lord Shiva, perched right on the edge of the Arabian Sea with a 20-storied Rajagopura temple tower.

Bengaluru

The garden city turned Silicon Valley of India, celebrated for year-round pleasant weather, expansive public parks, and a buzzing dining scene.

Historical Places

Walk through centuries of dynastic grandeur, architecture, and valour

14th – 17th Century

Hampi — Vijayanagara Empire

The bustling heart of the Vijayanagara Empire, featuring the sacred Virupaksha Temple, the monolithic Sasivekalu Ganesha, and the legendary musical stone pillars of Vittala Temple.

6th – 8th Century

Badami, Aihole & Pattadakal

The birthplace of South Indian temple architecture. Features Badami's cliff-cut red sandstone caves and the UNESCO-listed stone complexes of Pattadakal built by the Chalukyas.

Built in 1912

Mysore Palace

Seat of the Wadiyar dynasty, showcasing Indo-Saracenic grandeur with stained-glass ceilings, carved rosewood doors, and glittering illumination with nearly 100,000 bulbs on festivals.

18th Century

Srirangapatna — Tipu Sultan's Fort

The island fortress of the "Tiger of Mysore." Features Daria Daulat Bagh, the ornamental summer palace adorned with intricate wall murals, and the historic battle ramparts.

12th Century

Belur & Halebidu

Pinnacles of Hoysala craftsmanship. Carved from soapstone with astronomical precision, depicting celestial dancers (shilabalikas), epics, and intricate filigree stonework.

15th Century

Bidar Fort

An imposing Deccan fortress built by the Bahmani Sultanate with triple-layered moats, Turkish baths, and the ornate Rangeen Mahal decorated with mother-of-pearl mosaics.

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Immerse yourself in misty summits and roaring cataracts of the Western Ghats

Waterfall

Jog Falls

📍 Shimoga District

One of India's tallest un-tiered plunge waterfalls, cascading 253 meters in four distinct torrents known as Raja, Roarer, Rocket, and Rani.

Hill Station

Chikmagalur

📍 Western Ghats

The coffee cradle of India, surrounded by the Baba Budan Giri mountain range and Mullayanagiri, the highest peak in Karnataka standing at 1,930m.

Waterfall

Abbey Falls

📍 Coorg (Kodagu)

A picturesque waterfall enveloped by lush spice and coffee plantations, where waters tumble over natural boulders viewed from a hanging bridge.

Hill Station

Nandi Hills

📍 Chikkaballapur

Perched 1,478 meters high near Bengaluru, celebrated for spectacular sea-of-clouds sunrises, ancient Chola shrines, and cool breezes.

Waterfall

Shivanasamudra Falls

📍 Mandya District

Twin waterfalls where the holy Kaveri river splits into Gaganachukki and Bharachukki, roaring through forested gorges in torrential power.

Hill Station

Kudremukh

📍 Chikmagalur District

Known for its distinctive horse-shaped summit, endless green rolling meadows, and pristine shola forest trails inside a protected National Park.

Famous Karnataka Foods

Authentic flavors from fiery coastal curries to melt-in-the-mouth royal sweets

Masala Dosa

Golden, crispy fermented rice-lentil crepe smeared with spicy red garlic chutney and stuffed with aromatic potato palya. Served with coconut chutney and piping hot sambar.

Must Try

Bisi Bele Bath

Traditional one-pot delicacy of rice, lentils, assorted vegetables, tamarind, and a rich 30-spice blend, garnished with generous ghee and crispy boondi.

Comfort Food

Ragi Mudde

Wholesome finger-millet dough dumplings, swallowed whole with spicy mutton chops, soppina saaru (greens curry), or rich bassaru. Nutritious and deeply traditional.

Traditional

Mysore Pak

Invented in the royal kitchens of the Mysore Palace using roasted gram flour, pure desi ghee, and sugar syrup. Melts delightfully on the tongue.

Royal Sweet

Jolada Rotti

Unleavened sorghum flatbread popular in North Karnataka, served with yennegai (stuffed baby brinjals), spicy garlic powder, and churned curd.

North Karnataka

Mangalore Fish Curry

A zesty coastal specialty made with fresh catch simmered in roasted byadgi chilies, coconut milk, coriander seeds, and tangy kokum.

Coastal Specialty

Kosambari

A crisp, protein-rich salad made from soaked moong dal, crunchy diced cucumber, freshly grated coconut, lemon, and aromatic mustard-seed tadka.

Festival Special

Filter Coffee

A timeless brew crafted with freshly roasted Chikmagalur coffee beans and chicory, frothed with boiled milk in classic stainless steel davara-tumblers.

Travel Information

Everything you need to effortlessly plan your trip to Karnataka

Plan Your Perfect Karnataka Journey

From heritage trails and hill trekking to coastal retreats, Karnataka welcomes you with unforgettable experiences.

✈️ How to Reach

  • By Air: Kempegowda International Airport (Bengaluru) connects worldwide. Mangaluru, Hubballi, Belagavi, and Mysuru provide domestic air connectivity.
  • By Rail: Comprehensive South Western Railway network connects Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hubballi, and coastal Konkan Railway to all major Indian cities.
  • By Road: Premium state highways and national corridors connect Karnataka. KSRTC operates comfortable Airavat Volvo buses across all routes.

📅 Best Time to Visit

  • October — February: Ideal weather across the entire state. Perfect for heritage walks, safari tours, and witnessing Mysore Dasara.
  • March — May: Pleasantly cool in hill retreats like Coorg, Chikmagalur, and Agumbe; warmer across northern plains.
  • June — September: Monsoon season brings roaring waterfalls and lush greenery along the Western Ghats.

🏨 Where to Stay

  • Bengaluru: Luxury business hotels and boutique stays around MG Road, Indiranagar, and Koramangala.
  • Coorg & Chikmagalur: Plantation homestays offering home-cooked Kodava and Malnad delicacies.
  • Hampi & Gokarna: Riverfront cottages, heritage guest houses, and relaxed beachside huts.

💡 Essential Travel Tips

  • Pack light breathable cottons for plains and beaches; bring warm layers for early mornings in Coorg and Chikmagalur.
  • Kannada is the state language; English and Hindi are widely understood in cities, transit hubs, and tourist sites.
  • Enjoy darshinis for quick, hygienic, and authentic breakfasts like idli-vada and crispy dosas.
  • Download the KSRTC official app or book on IRCTC for hassle-free intercity bus and train travel.
  • Follow local temple etiquette — dress modestly covering shoulders and knees when visiting religious sanctums.

📍 Suggested Itineraries

  • 3-Day Weekend: Bengaluru → Heritage Mysuru → Coffee estates of Coorg.
  • 5-Day Heritage Trail: Bengaluru → Hampi Ruins → Badami Caves → Pattadakal.
  • 7-Day Nature & Coast: Chikmagalur Hills → Jog Falls → Murudeshwar → Gokarna Beaches.
  • 10-Day Grand Circuit: Complete cross-state journey covering history, forests, and the Arabian Sea.

🚗 Local Transportation

  • Ride-Hailing: Ola, Uber, and app-based auto rickshaws are readily available in Bengaluru and Mysuru.
  • Scenic Trains: The Vande Bharat and Shatabdi Express offer smooth 2-hour rides between Bengaluru and Mysuru.
  • Car Rentals: Self-drive services and private taxi rentals are ideal for navigating winding hill station roads.
  • Local Ferries: River coracle boats in Hampi and coastal ferries offer charming local transit.
